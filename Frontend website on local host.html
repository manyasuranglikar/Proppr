1. Lead Ingestion & Data Standardization

  • Inbound Webhook Triggers: Direct RESTful HTTPS Webhooks listen for payloads from Website form submissions, Meta Graph API LeadGen webhooks, and WhatsApp Business API gateways (Interakt, Wati, Gupshup).
  • Payload Normalization:
    • phone_number: Strips whitespace, hyphens, and standardizes +91 or leading 0 into canonical 10-digit format (e.g., 9876543210).
    • budget: Parsed by our regex parser into integer INR (e.g. ₹1.5 Cr → 15,000,000; ₹90 Lakh → 9,000,000).
    • enquiry_date: Stamped with UTC ISO timestamp and local IST timestamp.
  • Zero-Loss Staging Buffer: Every raw payload is immediately appended to an audit Google Sheet (Raw_Ingestion_Audit) before calling external CRM APIs. If CRM is down, no lead is ever lost.

2. Deduplication Strategy

  • Unique Identifier: Normalized 10-digit phone number is the primary key (supplemented by email if available).
  • The "Search-Before-Insert" Pattern:
    • Before creating a CRM Contact, query CRM via GET /contacts/search?phone={phone}.
    • If Exists: Do NOT create a duplicate contact. Instead, create an Engagement Note / Activity under the existing contact ID (e.g. "Repeat enquiry from Advertisement on 2026-10-28 for Gurgaon").
    • Increment touchpoint_count and update last_enquiry_date.
    • Alert the already assigned sales representative so they can follow up with updated context.

3. Team Notification & Smart Routing

  • Location-Based Routing:
    • Goa: Routed to Goa Holiday & Luxury Advisory Desk (Slack channel #leads-goa).
    • Gurgaon: Routed based on budget tier.
  • High-Priority Gurgaon VIP Routing:
    • Condition: Budget ≥ ₹1.5 Cr AND Location == 'Gurgaon'.
    • Tagged with Priority: HIGH, SLA: 15 Minutes.
    • Instant alert to #leads-gurgaon-vip tagging Senior Brokers + SMS/WhatsApp notification directly to the on-duty team lead.
  • Automated Customer Acknowledgement:
    • Sends instant WhatsApp greeting: "Hi Rahul, thank you for reaching out to Proppr regarding Gurgaon luxury properties. Our Senior Advisor has been assigned to your enquiry..."

4. Error Handling & Failure Resilience

  • Dead Letter Queue (DLQ): Any failing node automatically diverts the execution to an Error Trigger node.
  • Payload Preservation: The failed payload, node name, and stack trace are appended to a Failed_Leads_DLQ Google Sheet.
  • Automated Retries: Exponential backoff retry policy (Attempt 1: 1 min, Attempt 2: 5 min, Attempt 3: 15 min).
  • Tech Alerting: Real-time Slack alert sent to #ops-automation-alerts with a one-click manual retry button for DevOps/Ops leads.

Q1: Which leads would you flag as potential duplicates, and why?

Answer: Lead 101 and Lead 103 are flagged as duplicates.

  • Why: Both enquiries share the exact same normalized phone number: 9876543210 and the same prospect name (Rahul Sharma).
  • Real Estate Context: Lead 101 submitted an enquiry through the Website, and later Lead 103 submitted an enquiry through an online Advertisement (e.g. Meta / Google Ads).
  • In high-involvement purchases like real estate, buyers frequently interact with multiple channels before finalizing a site visit. Treating Lead 103 as a new contact would result in two different sales agents calling the same client, leading to a fragmented customer experience and internal commission conflicts.

Q2: How would you ensure duplicate enquiries do not create unnecessary CRM records?

Answer: Implementing the "Search-Before-Insert / Upsert Pattern" with an architectural separation between Contacts and Enquiries/Activities:

  1. Phone Number as Unique Key: Phone number is indexed with a unique constraint in the CRM Contact object.
  2. Lookup Step: When a new enquiry arrives, the workflow executes a lookup: 
    contact = crm.searchContactByPhone(normalized_phone)
  3. Forking Logic:
    • If Not Found: Create a new Contact record with status New.
    • If Found: Do not insert a new Contact. Instead:
      1. Append an Enquiry Note / Timeline Activity to the existing Contact: "Repeat enquiry received via Advertisement on 2026-10-28 for Gurgaon (₹1.5 Cr)".
      2. Increment touchpoint_count and update last_enquiry_date.
      3. Check current Contact status: If status is Closed - Lost or inactive > 30 days, re-open status to Re-engaged. If actively in progress, leave status intact and send a Slack notification to the assigned advisor.

Q3: If a lead has a budget of ₹1.5 Cr or above and is interested in Gurgaon, how would you flag it for priority follow-up?

Answer: Numerical Currency Parsing + Conditional Routing:

  1. Budget Normalization: Real estate budgets arrive in colloquial formats (₹1.5 Cr, ₹90 Lakh, ₹2 Cr). We parse these into standard numerical INR integers:
    • ₹1.5 Cr → 15,000,000
    • ₹90 Lakh → 9,000,000
    • ₹2 Cr → 20,000,000
  2. Filter Evaluation: 
    is_priority = (budget_inr >= 15000000) && (location.toLowerCase() == "gurgaon")
  3. Outcomes for Sample Leads:
    • Lead 101 (Rahul Sharma): Gurgaon + 15,000,000 → HIGH PRIORITY VIP
    • Lead 102 (Priya Mehta): Goa + 9,000,000 → Standard (Goa Desk)
    • Lead 103 (Rahul Sharma): Duplicate of 101 → Reinforced priority touchpoint
    • Lead 104 (Amit Verma): Gurgaon + 20,000,000 → HIGH PRIORITY VIP
  4. Automated Actions on Priority Flag:
    • CRM field Priority = "HIGH", SLA_Target = "15 Minutes".
    • Urgent alert to Slack channel #leads-gurgaon-vip tagging the Senior Advisory team.
    • Automated VIP SMS / WhatsApp acknowledgement sent to the lead within 60 seconds.

Q4: What would you do if the CRM was unavailable when a new lead arrived?

Answer: Decoupled Staging Buffer + Circuit Breaker + Replay Queue:

  1. Asynchronous Ingestion (Never synchronous point-to-point):
    • When an ad or webhook fires, the webhook listener immediately responds with HTTP 200 / 202 Accepted so Meta/Google/WhatsApp do not terminate or retry aggressively.
    • The raw payload is written immediately to a reliable persistence layer (e.g., Google Sheets Staging Log, Redis, or AWS SQS) with sync status PENDING_CRM_SYNC.
  2. Circuit Breaker & Exponential Backoff:
    • If the CRM responds with HTTP 500, 503, or connection timeout, the automation engine intercepts the error and schedules automated retries (e.g., Retry 1 after 1m, Retry 2 after 5m, Retry 3 after 15m).
  3. Dead Letter Queue (DLQ) & Reconciliation Cron:
    • If CRM remains down after max retries, the record is flagged in the Failed_Leads_DLQ sheet and a high-priority alert is sent to #tech-ops-alerts.
    • A scheduled reconciliation cron job runs every 30 minutes to check CRM health; as soon as CRM returns HTTP 200, it automatically replays and syncs all pending records in FIFO order. Zero leads are lost.

Q5: How would you prevent a failed workflow from processing the same lead multiple times?

Answer: Idempotency Keys + Checkpointing State Machine:

  1. Deterministic Idempotency Key:
    • Every lead is tagged with a unique hash: 
      idempotency_key = SHA256(phone + source + enquiry_date)
    • For Meta Ads or WhatsApp, we utilize the provider's native unique ID (e.g. Meta leadgen_id or WhatsApp message_id).
  2. Atomic State Tracking (Checkpointing):
    • Each processing stage updates an explicit state: 
      [INGESTED] → [BUFFERED] → [CRM_SYNCED] → [NOTIFIED] → [COMPLETED]
  3. Pre-Execution Guard:
    • Before creating a CRM contact or sending a client WhatsApp/SMS, the workflow queries the state store for idempotency_key. If status is already COMPLETED or CRM_SYNCED, execution exits gracefully without duplicating side-effects.
    • If a workflow fails halfway (e.g., written to Google Sheets, but CRM timeout), the retry mechanism resumes only from the uncompleted stage (CRM sync), preventing duplicate rows in Google Sheets or multiple greeting messages to the client.

# Excerpt from scripts/lead_processor.py
import re
from typing import Dict, List, Tuple, Any

def normalize_phone(phone_raw: Any) -> str:
    """Standardizes Indian phone numbers into canonical 10-digit format."""
    digits = re.sub(r"\D", "", str(phone_raw))
    if len(digits) == 12 and digits.startswith("91"):
        digits = digits[2:]
    elif len(digits) == 11 and digits.startswith("0"):
        digits = digits[1:]
    return digits

def parse_indian_budget(budget_str: Any) -> float:
    """Parses ₹1.5 Cr, ₹90 Lakh, ₹2 Cr into numerical INR values."""
    text = str(budget_str).strip().lower()
    num_match = re.search(r"(\d+(?:\.\d+)?)", text)
    if not num_match:
        return 0.0
    value = float(num_match.group(1))
    if "cr" in text or "crore" in text:
        return value * 10_000_000
    elif "lakh" in text or "lac" in text or "l" in text:
        return value * 100_000
    return value

def evaluate_priority(location: str, budget_inr: float) -> Tuple[bool, str]:
    """Flag: Budget >= ₹1.5 Cr (15M INR) AND Location is 'Gurgaon'."""
    is_gurgaon = location.strip().lower() == "gurgaon"
    meets_budget = budget_inr >= 15_000_000
    if is_gurgaon and meets_budget:
        return True, "HIGH PRIORITY (Gurgaon >= ₹1.5 Cr)"
    return False, "STANDARD"

Team Guide: How Proppr's Lead Automation Works

A simple, jargon-free operational handbook prepared for real estate advisors, sales executives, and operations managers.

1. What the Automation Does

Think of this automation as your 24/7 Digital Concierge:

  • Zero Manual Data Entry: Whenever a prospective property buyer reaches out—whether they fill a form on our website, message us on WhatsApp, or click one of our Facebook/Instagram ads—their details are instantly captured without anyone needing to copy-paste.
  • Smart Deduplication: If a customer enquired yesterday on our website and clicks an advertisement today, the system recognizes them by their phone number. It doesn't create a confusing second record or assign them to a different broker; it adds an update to their existing profile so you know they are highly interested.
  • Instant VIP Prioritization: When an enquiry comes in for Gurgaon with a budget of ₹1.5 Crore or above, the system flags it as High Priority, alerts the Gurgaon Senior Advisory desk immediately, and sends a warm acknowledgement message to the buyer in seconds.

2. How the Team Will Use It Day-to-Day

Your daily routine becomes smooth, fast, and organized:

  • Instant Slack / WhatsApp Alerts: You will receive a message in your team channel (e.g. #leads-gurgaon or #leads-goa) the exact second a lead submits an enquiry, complete with their name, budget, and direct CRM link.
  • Clear Lead Status Lifecycle:
    • New: Brand new lead waiting for your first call.
    • Contacted: You have called or messaged them.
    • Qualified: Buyer intent, location preference, and budget are confirmed.
    • Site Visit Scheduled: Site tour booked.
    • Closed: Deal completed or closed.
  • No Client Collisions: Since each phone number is unique, you will never have two advisors calling the same client by mistake.

3. What Happens When Something Goes Wrong

You can rest easy knowing that no lead will ever slip through the cracks:

  • Safety Backup Sheet: Every enquiry is instantly written to a backup Google Sheet the moment it arrives. Even if our CRM or internet experiences a temporary outage, your leads are 100% safe.
  • Automatic Retries: If the CRM system is busy or temporarily offline, our automation silently waits and automatically tries sending the lead again after 1 minute, 5 minutes, and 15 minutes.
  • Instant Ops Alert: If an error still occurs, our technical operations channel is notified immediately with the lead's details so an advisor can manually call them right away while the tech team investigates.

4. Future Improvements & Next Steps

Exciting enhancements we can add in Phase 2:

  • AI WhatsApp Concierge: An interactive AI assistant on WhatsApp that instantly qualifies buyers (e.g., asking "Are you looking for 3BHK or 4BHK?" or "Ready to move or under construction?") before routing to an advisor.
  • Automated Property Shortlisting: Automatically send matching Gurgaon or Goa property brochures as a branded PDF over WhatsApp based on their exact budget and location.
  • Round-Robin Auto-Assignment: Fairly distribute standard leads among sales reps based on working hours and current active lead capacity.
  • Site Visit Scheduling Calendar: Allow buyers to pick a site visit slot directly from a WhatsApp interactive button.
